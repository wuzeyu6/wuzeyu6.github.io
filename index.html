About

I am a Master's student in Data Science (Artificial Intelligence Applications) at the University of Macau, advised by Prof. Derek F. Wong in NLP²CT Lab, where I mainly work with Dr. Junchao Wu. My research focuses on efficient post-training of LLMs and trustworthy AI. Previously, I worked as a Research Assistant at Shenzhen Technology University, advised by Prof. Hang Yin, where I worked on smart agriculture and industrial applications.
